Wayly

Your time. Your place. Your plan.

A web app that suggests what to do in a city based on free time, budget and preferences, with explainable recommendations you can compare.

View repository All projects

01 The problem

Having two free hours in a city and no idea how to spend them without opening ten tabs.

02 What I built

  • Angular 18 with standalone components and strict TypeScript.
  • Explainable suggestion engine over a typed, swappable catalogue.
  • Optional Leaflet map that only requests tiles when coordinates are valid.
  • Design system with tokens and documented accessible states.
  • Jest, ESLint and 8 ADRs; zero-cost principle.

Transparency: the status above reflects the repository README. Anything planned is labelled as such; the full code and documentation are on GitHub.
